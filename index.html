Pa Joseph Ajibade Oyakale

1931 – 24th September 2026

Forever loved. Forever remembered. Forever cherished.

“Baba, you came, you fought, and indeed, you conquered.”

With profound gratitude to God and deep sorrow in our hearts, we celebrate the life of our beloved father, grandfather, great-grandfather, mentor and friend, Pa Joseph Ajibade Oyakale.

Baba lived a long and purposeful life. He passed away on 24th September 2026, leaving behind a family and community shaped by his hard work, values, sacrifices and love.

His Life Story

Baba was born in 1931 at Sese’s Compound, Inisa, Odo Otin Local Government Area, Osun State, Nigeria. He was the son of Mr Oyatola and Mrs Irele, who hailed from Ile Oree in Inisa.

As a young man, Baba travelled to Ghana, then the Gold Coast, in pursuit of opportunity and a better future. He lived in Accra, Kotokpo, Kumasi and Asikuma, where he engaged in apprenticeship, the sale of clothing materials and truck driving. During his time in Ghana, he stayed with his late uncles, Pa Raphael Oyadiji and Baba Adeyefa.

His years in Ghana broadened his experience and outlook. Through home schooling and everyday life, he learned to read and write in Yoruba, acquired English and became fluent in Ana, a Ghanaian language.

Baba eventually returned to Nigeria, where he devoted himself to family, farming and honest work. Following the death of his elder brother, Pa Oyakale Tardius, in 1966, he took on significant family responsibilities. He later settled in Alabameta, near Ile-Ife, where he spent about 40 years as a hardworking farmer.

Baba also married his late brother’s wife, Mama Theressa Abeni, of blessed memory, and continued to carry the responsibilities of family with commitment and care.

A Father Who Invested in People

Baba believed in hard work, discipline, responsibility and education. Although he was a farmer who worked with his hands, he made sacrifices to give his children opportunities to learn and build meaningful lives.

That investment continues to bear fruit. Among his children and grandchildren are CEOs, business tycoons, entrepreneurs, professionals and other stakeholders contributing to business and society in different fields.

A Man of Character and Faith

  • Hardworking and industrious
  • Disciplined and responsible
  • Truthful and straightforward
  • Prudent and resourceful
  • Caring and family-oriented
  • A devoted Christian and communicant

Baba was known for his strong sense of duty, practical wisdom and commitment to his family. He lived by the principle that honest work, perseverance and responsibility matter.

His Legacy

Baba’s legacy is found not only in the years he lived, but in the people and generations he helped shape. His children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren carry forward the lessons of hard work, courage, discipline, faith and family responsibility.

The fruits of his labour continue to speak through the lives of those he raised, guided and encouraged.

Good Night, Baba

Baba, you came. You fought. You worked. You sacrificed. You endured. You raised your family and invested in generations. And indeed, you conquered.

We thank God for the gift of your life and for the lasting legacy you leave behind.

Rest peacefully, our beloved Baba.

May your gentle soul rest in perfect peace. Amen.
