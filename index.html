Pa Joseph Ajibade Oyakale

1931 – 23rd September 2026

Forever loved. Forever remembered. Forever cherished.

“Baba, you came, you fought, and indeed, you conquered.”

With profound gratitude to God and deep sorrow in our hearts, we celebrate the remarkable life and legacy of our beloved father, grandfather, great-grandfather, mentor and friend, Pa Joseph Ajibade Oyakale.

Baba lived a long, purposeful and fulfilled life and passed away on 23rd September 2026 at the age of 95.

His Life Story

Baba was born in 1931 at Sese’s Compound, Inisa, Odo Otin Local Government Area, Osun State, Nigeria, to his late parents, Mr Oyakale and Mrs Irele. His mother hailed from Ile Oree in Inisa.

As a young man, Baba travelled to the Gold Coast, now Ghana, in pursuit of business opportunities. He engaged in apprenticeship, the sale of clothing materials and truck driving. He stayed with his late uncles, Pa Raphael Oyadiji and Baba Adeyefa.

While in Ghana, he attended home schooling and learned to read and write in Yoruba, acquired some English and became fluent in Ana, a Ghanaian language. He lived in Kumasi, Accra and Kotopo.

In 1955, he returned to Nigeria and married his beloved wife, Mama Maria Oguntoyibo of Oke Aya, Inisa. They later returned to Ghana before eventually settling permanently in Nigeria.

Following the death of his eldest brother, Pa Oyakale Tardius, in 1966, Baba returned to Nigeria and took responsibility for the family. He later moved to Alabameta, near Ile-Ife, where he spent about 40 years as a hardworking farmer.

A Father Who Valued Education

Baba believed strongly in hard work, discipline and education. Although he was a farmer who worked with his hands, he made great sacrifices to ensure that his children were educated.

His investment in education continues to bear fruit through his children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren, among whom are pastors, lawyers, doctors, accountants, engineers, bankers and other professionals.

A Man of Character and Faith

  • Hardworking and industrious
  • Disciplined and responsible
  • Truthful and straightforward
  • Prudent and resourceful
  • Caring and family-oriented
  • A devoted Christian and communicant

Baba was a disciplinarian and a man who valued honest work, family responsibility and service. He was a true “jack of all trades and master of many.”

His Legacy

Baba was blessed with children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren. His greatest legacy is the lives he touched and the generations that continue to carry his name, values and teachings forward.

His hands may now be still, but the fruits of his labour continue to speak. His physical presence may be gone, but his legacy lives on.

Good Night, Baba

Baba, you came. You fought. You worked. You sacrificed. You endured. You raised your family. You invested in generations. And indeed, you conquered.

We thank God for the gift of your life, the years He gave you, the family you raised and the legacy you left behind.

Rest peacefully, our beloved Baba.

May your gentle soul rest in perfect peace. Amen.
